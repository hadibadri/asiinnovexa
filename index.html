How an engagement runs

Swipe through the four steps.

  1. 1

    Discover

    A twenty-minute call to understand the outcome you need, the deadline and what decides it. You leave with a clear yes, no, or a better route.

  2. 2

    Align

    Scope, agreement and a named team. The people you meet before signing are the people accountable after it.

  3. 3

    Deliver

    Short cycles with internal review before anything reaches you: a shortlist, a proposal volume, a release. Progress you can see every week.

  4. 4

    Sustain

    We stay after go-live and after placement: follow-ups with new hires, maintenance for systems, and a lessons-learned review for the next bid.
